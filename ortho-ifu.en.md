# Orthopedic Implant IFU · English

> CC BY 4.0 · Contributor @stevenzhao07 (MedXpert) · Template, not a product-specific label.

## Product Identification
Total knee arthroplasty implant (femoral/tibial components), example model ORTHO-TKA-001; see label for UDI-DI.

## Intended Use
For total knee arthroplasty in end-stage knee osteoarthritis to restore mobility and weight-bearing.

## Contraindications
Active infection; insufficient bone stock for primary stability; confirmed allergy to implant materials.

## Warnings
Non-sterile components must be sterilized per IFU; assess bone defects and soft tissue in revision cases.

## Cleaning & Sterilization
Clean and sterilize non-sterile components per manufacturer IFU where applicable; reusable instruments follow clean-disinfect-sterilize.

## Traceability
Record lot/UDI, surgery date, surgeon and patient identifiers in the hospital traceability system.

## Adverse Events
Report per jurisdiction (NMPA/FDA/EU MDR/PMDA); follow up on abnormal wear or loosening.
